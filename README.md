# USB-OTG-Scanner-Pro
USB OTG Scanner Pro is a 100% free, lightweight Android application designed to bridge the gap between Android smartphones and legacy USB multi-function printers and scanners. By connecting your phone directly to a printer via a standard USB OTG (On-The-Go) cable, the app allows you to scan documents, receipts, and photos in high resolution directly into JPEG, PNG, WebP, or Multi-Page PDF files without needing a PC, Wi-Fi router, or official manufacturer cloud apps. It features a built-in interactive cropping tool, page rotation utilities, and an automated protocol detector that communicates with your hardware instantly.

🖨️ Which Printers Work With This App?
Because the app implements native industry-standard scanning protocols over USB host communication, it supports a wide variety of multi-function printers and flatbed scanners:
HP Printers / Scanners: All models supporting HP LEDM or Legacy HP SCL protocols (such as the HP DeskJet 1510 series, HP DeskJet Ink Advantage series, HP LaserJet multi-function units, and OfficeJet models).

Canon Printers / Scanners: Most Canon PIXMA all-in-one multi-function printers utilizing USB bulk scanner endpoints.

Epson Printers / Scanners: Models supporting Epson ESC/I or Epson eSCL/Direct imaging protocols (such as Epson EcoTank and Workforce multi-function printers).

Universal eSCL / Mopria Scanners: Any modern network or USB scanner that speaks the open eSCL standard.

General Requirement: Your printer/scanner must have a USB port and be connected to your phone using a working USB OTG adapter (Type-C or Micro-USB depending on your phone) paired with a standard USB printer cable (USB-A to USB-B).

Users can capture crisp scans at resolutions up to 600 DPI in color or grayscale, preview pages instantly, rotate orientations, and use a precision interactive cropping tool with grid overlays to trim unwanted borders. Furthermore, it includes a robust Multi-Page PDF document builder, allowing multiple scanned pages to be queued and compiled into a single clean PDF file saved directly to the device’s Downloads folder.
