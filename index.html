About the App

USB OTG Scanner Pro is a 100% free Android utility that bridges the gap between your smartphone and wired USB flatbed scanners or multi-function printers. Connect via a standard USB OTG cable to scan crisp documents directly into JPEG, PNG, WebP, or Multi-Page PDF files without requiring a PC, Wi-Fi router, or proprietary cloud applications.

Key Features

  • Plug & Play OTG: Direct USB host communication with automatic protocol detection (HP LEDM, Universal eSCL, Canon PIXMA, Epson ESC/I, and Legacy HP SCL).
  • High-Resolution Scanning: Capture scans up to 600 DPI in full color (24-bit) or grayscale (8-bit).
  • Built-in Document Editing: Rotate pages, crop edges precisely with visual grid overlays, and build Multi-Page PDFs.
  • 100% Free & Secure: No hidden subscriptions. Fully protected via R8 code obfuscation.

Supported Printer Brands

Works seamlessly with multi-function printers and scanners from HP, Canon, Epson, Brother, and Samsung that support standard USB printer/scanner classes or eSCL/LEDM protocols.
